Product Update 21

Pauline Pham
-February 9, 2026
Hi there,
We've shipped some of our most powerful updates yet. Your agents can now learn new skills instantly (like Neo downloading kung fu), and you can mention teammates to bring humans into any conversation. Frames export to PDF, new MCP tools connect you to Gmail, UKG Ready, Snowflake, Productboard, Vanta, and Guru, plus our image generation leveled up to 2K and 4K resolution.
Here's everything that's new in Dust.

🤺 It’s called having skills

Skills transform how you build agents. Package instructions, knowledge, and tools once. Any agent in your workspace can use them instantly. Update a skill, and every agent using it improves automatically.
Think of it like Neo in the Matrix: "I know kung fu."
Instead of copying the same instructions across multiple agents, Skills let you capture your teams' expertise once and distribute it everywhere, with no rebuilding required. Perfect for teams scaling agentic workflows while maintaining consistency.
📖 Read the guide and our post

👋 Mentions: Collaborate better, together

Your agents now work alongside your teammates. Mention anyone in a conversation using @, and they'll get notified instantly. Agents can mention team members when they need input. Your inbox keeps you on top of what matters.
No more copying links, forwarding messages, or wondering if someone saw your question. Mentions bring humans and AI together seamlessly: turning Dust conversations into true cross-functional collaboration.
📖 Read the full blog post

💾 PDF your Frame

You can now export any Frame as a PDF with portrait or landscape orientation. Enterprise plans get clean PDFs with no footer. 😎
Perfect for sharing reports, distributing to external stakeholders, or confidently presenting your Dust work outside the platform.

🧮 The MCP corner

Gmail can now send mail! — The Gmail MCP includes a new tool: send mail. It can now send emails directly. Disabled by default on existing workspaces to ensure nothing breaks. To enable, go to Spaces > Tools > Gmail > Send Mail. Enabled by default for all new Gmail tool installations.
Snowflake — Connect Snowflake with personal OAuth authentication. Each user authenticates individually, meaning users can only access through Dust what they already have permission to access in Snowflake. This feature is available for enterprise customers only. Reach out to enable it for your workspace.
Productboard — Connect your workspace to Productboard to capture feedback, manage product hierarchy, and sync product workspace and individual data from Dust agents. Read the docs
Vanta — Unlock automated test status, vulnerability tracking, risk scenarios, frameworks, control evidence, and more using Vanta in Dust. Read the docs
Guru — Let your agents search, reference, and interact with your Guru cards and collections.
UKG Ready — Request, view, and manage PTO directly from Dust. Read the docs

➕ And more...

More Powerful Image Generation — Upgraded to gemini-3-pro-image (also known as Nano Banana 2) with higher quality image generation from 1K to 2K or 4K resolution. You can now include up to 14 reference images in your composition to edit assets or compose new ones. Get started with examples
Per API-key usage cap — Admins can now set a maximum dollar amount that an API key is allowed to spend on a 30-day rolling basis for more granular control over credits usage. This feature is available for enterprise customers only.
We'd love your feedback on these new features and tools, and any suggestions for future improvements, ping us at [email protected].
