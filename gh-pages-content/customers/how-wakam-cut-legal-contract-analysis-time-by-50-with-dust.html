How Wakam cut legal contract analysis time by 50% with Dust

About Wakam

Wakam is a B2B2C insurance company that designs custom insurance solutions distributed as white-label products through its Play & Plug® technology platform. Their solutions reach customers through 100 distributor partners across 32 countries. With a turnover exceeding €836 million in 2024, Wakam has established itself as the European leader in digital and embedded insurance.
As a mission-driven company employing 250 people across 5 European countries, Wakam operates with the purpose of "making insurance transparent and impactful." The company has built its corporate culture around 11 cultural markers that promote collaboration, curiosity, learning, open-mindedness, and innovation.
Interested in learning more about how Dust can help your Legal team? Visit our legal page or reach out to our sales team.

Challenge: Manually processing complex B2B contracts cost time

Due to the nature of the insurance market Wakam operates in, the company handles a vast number of legal contracts, each requiring high-stakes work and precision. Wakam's legal team was spending hours on repetitive contract work, reviewing terms, analyzing clauses, and generating complex contracts. This created several operational challenges, such as:
  • Document processing was painful, with the legal team spending hours manually extracting key terms, conditions, deadlines, and obligations from contracts.
  • Knowledge was scattered across multiple systems, making it difficult to quickly locate relevant legal precedents and clauses.
  • Verifying compliance was slow and created significant delays in contract approval workflow.
Since Wakam operates across multiple countries, their legal team constantly had to work with documents in both English and French, which just added another layer of complexity to an already complicated process.
The legal team was losing time on repetitive manual tasks instead of focusing on the strategic legal work that actually moved the business forward.

The solution: an AI-powered legal department

Wakam first tried to build their own AI solution from December 2023 to June 2024 but the team realized this approach wasn't fast enough. After evaluating various options, Wakam selected Dust based on the following factors:
  • Since Dust is based in France, they naturally understood Wakam's European data sovereignty requirements. There was no concern about where their sensitive legal data was going.
  • The platform came with enterprise-grade security and compliance capabilities built right in, which was non-negotiable when dealing with confidential legal documents.
  • Dust provided technical support during implementation, accelerating the adoption process.
  • Dust could already do all the use cases Wakam had identified and it integrated well with multiple systems, so no need to build it all from scratch.
To speed the Dust implementation up and spark innovation, Wakam started running regular hackathons where teams could explore new ways to use AI and build specialized agents. The legal department leveraged these events to work directly with Wakam’sI tech teams to build agents that solved their specific problems.
Here are 3 specific agents developed by Wakam to enhance their legal operations.

Agent LegalContracts

This is a specialized AI agent designed specifically for legal document work like creating, analyzing, optimizing, and translating contracts. It completely changed how Wakam's legal department handles contracts, making everything faster and more accurate while keeping compliance tight.
The agent integrates directly with Wakam's SharePoint legal document repository, providing immediate access to the complete contract database. The legal team can access the agent through the Dust Chrome extension while working in SharePoint, with no need to switch between platforms.
Key agent capabilities:
Document analysis and simplification
  • Takes complex legal jargon and translates it into plain English (while keeping the legal meaning intact)
  • Pulls out the critical stuff – pricing, payment terms, service level agreements
  • Creates timelines of all deadlines and key dates so nothing falls through the cracks
  • Tracks changes between different versions of contracts so you don't have to do tedious line-by-line comparisons
  • Spot connections between related documents so you get the full picture of complex deals
Contract creation and editing
  • Writes new contract clauses or suggests changes to existing ones
  • Creates first drafts of new contracts using established templates that follow Wakam's standards
  • Translates contract sections between English and French while preserving the precise legal meaning
  • Rewrites policy text to keep the same legal effect but use different language (helpful for avoiding copyright issues)
Compliance and quality control
  • Verify contract aligns with regulatory requirements across multiple countries
  • Flags potential compliance issues and suggests alternatives that actually work
  • Identifies potentially problematic clauses based on consumer protection regulations
  • Makes sure legal terminology is used consistently throughout documents
  • Catches contradictions or inconsistencies before contracts get finalized
“The integration of Dust AI into our workflow creates an effective human-AI collaboration model where the agent handles routine aspects of the legal work while our legal professionals focus on strategic decisions and complex negotiations.” Cédrick MARSAUX, Chief Legal Officer @ Wakam

Agent ContractDataExtractor

This agent connects to Wakam's complete contract database and uses Dust's "extract data" tool (= specialized way of searching enabled by Dust that allows people to parse through large documentation and get around context windows which are a natural limitation of LLMs. Without using a tool like extract, the model wouldn’t be necessarily able to ensure it kept the legal meaning intact because context might be dropped.) to manage legal documents. It automatically processes contracts to extract critical information that would otherwise require hours of manual review.
Key agent capabilities:
Comprehensive data extraction: The agent automatically identifies and extracts essential contractual elements, including:
  • Deadlines, KPIs, and service level agreements (SLAs)
  • Financial terms, including pricing, payment schedules, and billing cycles
  • Key milestone dates and operational deadlines
  • Termination conditions and required notice periods
  • Liability clauses and risk allocation terms
Report generation and analysis:
  • Creates detailed executive summaries highlighting critical contract terms
  • Generates comparison reports between different contract versions
  • Identifies potential risks and obligations across contract portfolios
  • Flag unusual terms or deviations from standard templates
Contract relationship mapping:
  • Shows how different contracts relate to each other (super helpful when you have complex partnerships with lots of moving parts)
  • Tracks dependencies across master agreements, amendments, and work orders
  • Gives the team a heads up when changing one contract might affect others

Agent LegalWatch

This agent monitors regulatory changes and industry developments across the multiple jurisdictions where Wakam operates. It uses Dust's web search capabilities to track trusted legal sources in specific geographical regions.
The agent automatically monitors for regulatory updates that could impact Wakam's business, eliminating the need for manual monitoring across multiple countries and ensuring the legal team stays informed of relevant changes.

Results: 50% reduction in contract analysis time

The impact of implementing these legal agents have been great for Wakam's legal operations:
  • Contract analysis time cut in half. The legal team can now process twice as many contracts in the same amount of time
  • Document search time reduced by 40-60%. Instead of spending hours hunting through multiple systems for relevant information, the agents can find what the team needs in minutes.
  • Knowledge sharing got so much better. Everyone has access to legal precedents and institutional knowledge that used to be buried in multiple folders. Legal documents use the same terminology, follow standardized templates, and meet higher quality and compliance standards across the board.
  • Contract reviews and approvals happen faster, compliance checking is more thorough, and working with documents in multiple languages isn't a headache anymore.
  • The legal team is more satisfied with their jobs. They're spending way less time on boring, repetitive tasks and way more time on the strategic and creative legal work that actually makes a difference for the business.
“Since the initial rollout, we have expanded the use of Dust throughout the company. We now have 130 AI agents powered by Dust working across different departments, making operations simpler and more efficient everywhere.” Etienne Debost, CTO @ Wakam
What's next: Wakam and Dust plan to work more together to integrate more deeply with their legal workflow systems so everything feels seamless. They're also planning to connect to more external legal and industry resources to make their agents even smarter. The long-term goal is to automate even more of the legal processes.
