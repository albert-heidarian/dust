Integrate AI in your everyday applications

10 min
Gaëlle Caplier
So far, we've only interacted with Dust agents directly from the Dust website. But you can also embed your agents directly in your day-to-day applications! For example, you can call your agents from your work messaging app (Slack or Teams), and you can also leverage them directly from your browser.

1. Call Dust agents from Slack or Teams

You can interact with your Dust agents directly from Slack or Teams. You just need to use the syntax @Dust +NameofAgent to call the agent you want, and it should reply directly to you!
Example of the syntax in Teams:

2. Use Dust in the browser extension

You can use Dust without even leaving your current webpage, thanks to the Dust Chrome extension. Install it from the Chrome app store and pin it to your browser for easy access!

3. In action - Using Dust anywhere

Conclusion

And there you go! You've taken your first steps on Dust, you know how to interact with your AI agents - both global agents and custom agents - directly on the Dust website, as well as in the browser extension and in your work messaging application. Next step will be creating your own agents, stay tuned!
